# MLAT Final-Test Governance Note

The 2025-01-01 through 2026-05-22 partition was displayed and used in earlier
research cycles. A new notebook does not restore its holdout status.

For MLAT Feature Research v1:

- Development and Validation are the only feature/outcome research samples.
- Final-test partition metadata may be loaded only to prove exclusion and
  artifact alignment.
- Final-test labels are not loaded into an MLAT evaluation frame.
- No Final-test distributions, ICs, buckets, model metrics, thresholds, or
  feature verdicts are computed or displayed.
- Feature definitions, parameters, transformations, and advancement rules are
  frozen before labels enter the MLAT pipeline.
- MGC remains locked until a separate transfer protocol is approved.

Genuine confirmation requires observations that were unavailable to the
research team when v1 was frozen. The recommended next holdout is a future
paper-trading/live-shadow period with a dated activation point, unchanged code
and hashes, and a one-time evaluation protocol.
