- **The transfer gap is structural, not a legacy-liquidity artifact.** Entry-price containment is 75.5-80.9 percent in every year of the sample, including 2025-26 when MGC minute coverage reaches 99.9 percent. About one in five GC first-contact prices never prints on MGC during the decision minute.
- MGC synchronization and liquidity at the exact bars this project would trade are excellent: 100 percent coverage, median 274 contracts per decision minute, no zero-volume minutes, median basis one tick.
- Consequence for any future execution mapping: a boundary-touch limit at the GC-derived price cannot be assumed filled on MGC. An MGC execution layer needs an MGC-native entry treatment - a declared tolerance band around the GC price or a marketable entry with modeled slippage - and its cost must be measured with real order telemetry in the Rithmic forward test, not assumed from bars.
